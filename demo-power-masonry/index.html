Why masonry fails around here

New England weather is hard on brick and stone. Three things cause most of what we get called about.

Freeze and thaw

Water gets into a hairline crack, freezes, and widens it. Every winter makes it worse.

The wrong mortar

Modern hard mortar on old soft brick cracks the brick instead of the joint. Common on repairs done cheap.

Water going the wrong way

Bad drainage or missing flashing puts water where it shouldn't be. Fix that first or it comes back.
